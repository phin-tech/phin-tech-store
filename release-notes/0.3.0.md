- Ads and box art now come from apps.phin.tech, so a new app gets its ad and its box
  without a new version of the store. The store downloads only the pictures it doesn't
  have, and keeps them for when you're offline.
- **View ▸ Reload the Shelves** (⌘R) also fetches new ads and art.
- The store keeps itself up to date: it checks once a day and installs a new version
  for the next time you open it. Turn that off in Settings.
